Data Deletion Instructions

Applies to Smaug Studios Publisher · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Smaug Studios Publisher stores only the minimum data needed to publish videos to connected accounts. That data is OAuth tokens, basic account details, and records of the videos we published. Our Privacy Policy describes it in full. You can have all of it deleted at any time, in either of two ways.

Option 1: Revoke access on the platform

Removing the App from your account stops all access right away. We then delete the stored tokens within 7 days and account details within 30 days.

  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Website permissions (or Apps and websites) → select Smaug Studios Publisher → Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Smaug Studios Publisher, then choose Remove access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions → Smaug Studios Publisher → Remove access.

Option 2: Email us

Email smaugstudios.official@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include the platform and the username or channel name of the account. We will:

  1. confirm we received your request within 3 business days;
  2. delete all tokens, account information and publishing records tied to that account within 30 days; and
  3. email you a confirmation code once deletion is finished.

Deleting data held by the App does not delete videos already published on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. Those posts are controlled in the platform's own app and can be removed there.
