Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smaug Studios ("Smaug Studios", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information in connection with Smaug Studios Publisher (the "App") and this website (together, the "Services").

The App is an internal publishing tool. It captures gameplay footage of Smaug Studios' own Roblox games, edits it into short videos, and publishes those videos to social media accounts owned by Smaug Studios through the official TikTok, YouTube and Instagram APIs. Only accounts that are owned and operated by Smaug Studios are connected to the App.

1. Information we collect

When a Smaug Studios account is connected to the App, the App receives the following through OAuth authorization. It receives only these items:

CategoryExamplesSource
Authorization credentialsOAuth access tokens and refresh tokens, granted scopes, token expiry timesTikTok, Google, Meta
Basic account informationAccount or channel ID, display name, username, profile picture URLTikTok, Google, Meta
Posting informationCreator posting settings (such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length), upload and publish status, IDs and URLs of videos we publishedTikTok, Google, Meta
Content we createGameplay footage of our own games, edited videos, titles, descriptions, hashtags and thumbnailsSmaug Studios
Technical logsTimestamps, API request results and error messages, kept so we can debug failed uploadsThe App

The App does not collect or access passwords, direct messages, contacts, followers' personal information, comments, other users' videos, payment information, precise location, or any information about players of our Roblox games. Gameplay is recorded in Roblox Studio using Smaug Studios' own test accounts, not from live game servers.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • authenticate the connected Smaug Studios account and keep it connected;
  • check which privacy levels and posting options the account supports before uploading;
  • upload and publish videos to the connected account and confirm whether each post succeeded;
  • show the connected account's name and avatar inside the App, so we can confirm where a video will be posted;
  • keep a record of which videos were published and where;
  • troubleshoot errors and keep the App secure; and
  • comply with legal obligations and platform policies.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, selling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing our own content.

3. Platform-specific disclosures

TikTok

The App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It requests the user.info.basic scope (account identifier, display name and avatar) and the video.publish and/or video.upload scopes (to post videos or send them to the TikTok inbox as drafts). Before posting, the App asks TikTok for the creator's posting settings and follows TikTok's rules on privacy levels, interaction settings and content disclosure. TikTok's own handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API v3). It requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope to upload videos and, where needed, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly to identify the connected channel and check upload status. By using the App you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles data.

Instagram (Meta)

The App uses the Instagram Graph API with the permissions needed to publish content to a connected Instagram professional account (such as instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish). Meta's handling of data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy. To request deletion of data the App holds, see our Data Deletion page.

4. Google API Services & Limited Use

Smaug Studios Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing upload feature described in this policy;
  • we do not transfer Google user data to others, except as needed to provide that feature, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice;
  • we do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it;
  • humans do not read Google user data, except with consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations; and
  • we do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

5. AI processing

The App uses AI agents to help capture and edit gameplay footage and to draft titles and captions. These tools process only content we create: our own gameplay footage and the text written about it. Data received from TikTok, Google or Meta (tokens, account details or posting information) is never sent to AI model providers. No platform data is used to train any AI model.

6. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade information. We share it only:

  • With the platform you post to. For example, videos and their captions are sent to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram when we publish them.
  • With service providers that host or run the App for us, such as cloud storage and AI editing services for our own footage. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Smaug Studios, our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, with notice and subject to this policy.

7. Storage & security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and stored only on systems controlled by Smaug Studios. All communication with platform APIs uses HTTPS. Access to the App and its credentials is limited to authorized Smaug Studios personnel. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

8. Retention & deletion

  • Access and refresh tokens are kept while the account stays connected and deleted within 7 days of disconnecting or revoking access.
  • Basic account information is deleted within 30 days of disconnecting.
  • Publishing records (video IDs, URLs, status) are kept for up to 12 months so we can track our own marketing, then deleted.
  • Technical logs are kept for up to 90 days.

You can request deletion at any time. See Data Deletion.

9. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, then remove Smaug Studios Publisher.
  • Instagram: open Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, then remove Smaug Studios Publisher.

Once access is revoked, the App can no longer use the account, and stored data is deleted as described in Section 8.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, or to object to or restrict how it is processed. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days and will not discriminate against you for using them.

11. Children

The App is a business tool operated by Smaug Studios. It is not directed to children and is not used by them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you think a child's information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. This website

This website is static. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms. Our hosting provider may record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address under the Google Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to this policy
