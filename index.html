Smaug Studios Publisher

Smaug Studios makes games on Roblox. Smaug Studios Publisher is our in-house tool for publishing short gameplay trailers of those games to Smaug Studios' own social media accounts.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What the app does

Smaug Studios Publisher automates how our trailers are made and posted. It captures gameplay footage of Smaug Studios games in Roblox Studio, edits it into short vertical videos, and uploads the finished videos to the studio's official accounts through each platform's official API.

1

Capture

Records gameplay of our own games inside Roblox Studio, using the studio's own test accounts. No footage of other players is captured.

2

Edit

Cuts the footage into short clips and adds captions, music and branding.

3

Review

Each video gets a title, description and hashtags. Before anything is posted, the studio can see which account it will be posted to and its privacy setting.

4

Publish

Uploads the video to the studio's connected accounts using official APIs and the permissions the account owner granted.

Connected platforms

The app posts only to accounts owned and operated by Smaug Studios. It does not access, collect or post to anyone else's account.

TikTok: Content Posting API YouTube: YouTube Data API v3 Instagram: Instagram Graph API

Your data, in short

We request only the permissions needed to upload videos and check whether a post succeeded. That means a basic account identifier (so we know which account we're posting to), video upload permission, and the status of our own uploads.

We never sell data, show ads, or use data from TikTok, Google or Meta to train AI models. Access tokens are stored encrypted, and access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy. To remove data, see Data Deletion.
